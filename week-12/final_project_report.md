## 1. Executive Summary
Over the course of 12 weeks, our team engineered an end-to-end data pipeline, predictive demand forecasting model, and business intelligence (BI) dashboard suite tailored for heavy supply chain operations. This project addressed critical supply chain bottlenecks, inventory distribution inefficiencies, and customer concentration vulnerabilities across multi-warehouse logistics networks.

## 2. Core Architectural & Analytical Modules
* **Data Ingestion & Cleaning (Weeks 1–4):** Standardized raw transactional logs, supplier records, and warehouse inventory databases, removing anomalies and structuring relational schemas.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis & RFM Segmentation (Weeks 5–8):** Segmented buyer behavior using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) modeling, identifying core champions and revenue drivers.
* **Predictive Forecasting & BI Dashboards (Weeks 9–11):** Deployed time-series predictive algorithms forecasting an 8% Q1 growth trajectory ($520,000 target) and built interactive executive dashboards.
* **Consolidation & Strategic Delivery (Week 12):** Finalized repository governance, executive reporting, and strategic operational roadmaps.

## 3. Key Findings Summary
* **Pareto Revenue Concentration:** The top 20% of catalog SKUs drive 74% of total revenue.
* **Key Account Dependency:** The top decile (10%) of customer accounts generates 58% of overall gross revenue, requiring dedicated retention strategies.
* **Warehouse Bottlenecks:** The Central Warehouse handles 45% of order volume and exhibits a 4.2% fulfillment delay rate, signaling an urgent need for inventory decentralization.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations
* Shift 15% of buffer stock for high-velocity SKUs from the Central Warehouse to regional hubs.
* Implement structured Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for top-tier corporate accounts to mitigate churn risk.
