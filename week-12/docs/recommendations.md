# Strategic Business Recommendations & Action Plan

## 1. Operational & Supply Chain Strategy
* **Stock Re-Allocation:** Decentralize Category A high-velocity inventory by shifting 15% of buffer stock to regional hubs to reduce Central Warehouse strain.
* **Lead Time Optimization:** Re-evaluate supplier lead-time thresholds to prevent safety-stock depletion during demand spikes.

## 2. Commercial & Account Management Strategy
* **Key Account Preservation:** Establish dedicated service-level agreements (SLAs) for the top 10% buyer decile to safeguard 58% of total revenue against churn.
* **Dormant Account Win-Back:** Initiate targeted pricing promotions for "At Risk" RFM customer segments.

## 3. Analytical Limitations & Future Roadmap
* **Data Granularity:** External macroeconomic indicators (e.g., freight index spikes, inflation rates) were not integrated into the predictive baseline.
* **Future Enhancement:** Transition static batch processing to real-time streaming pipeline processing using Apache Kafka and PySpark.
