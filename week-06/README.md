# Week 06: Warehouse Operations & Efficiency Analysis

## Sprint Goal
Analyze warehouse operations, throughput, capacity, and product distribution to identify efficiency issues and potential bottlenecks.

## Workflow Pipeline
`Raw Branch & Sales Data` -> `01_warehouse_efficiency.ipynb` -> `Workload & Throughput Aggregation` -> `Visualizations & Bottleneck Identification`

## Objectives
* Prepare and analyze warehouse and branch operational data
* Calculate workload (Orders) and operational throughput (Units) per location
* Compare revenue handled and product variety across warehouses
* Evaluate workload balance across facilities to locate operational bottlenecks
* Identify underperforming or overloaded warehouses requiring intervention
