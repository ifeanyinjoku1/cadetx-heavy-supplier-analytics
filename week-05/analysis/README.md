# Week 5 — Analysis Outputs

This folder contains the analytical datasets generated during the Week 5 inventory analysis.

## Files

- `product_stock_requirements.csv` — Product-level safety stock, reorder level, maximum stock level, lead time, and related stock requirements.
- `inventory_health_summary.csv` — Combined product movement and inventory requirement analysis.
- `abc_classification.csv` — ABC classification based on maximum stock value.
- `week5_inventory_kpis.csv` — Summary of key Week 5 inventory KPIs.

## Main Analysis

The analysis covers:

- Safety stock requirements
- Reorder levels
- Maximum stock levels
- Stock requirement values
- Lead times
- Product movement classification
- ABC/Pareto classification
- Sales movement versus stock requirements
