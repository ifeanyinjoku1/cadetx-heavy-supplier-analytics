# Week 08: Customer & Sales Performance Analysis

## Sprint Goal
Analyze customer and sales performance to identify key customer segments, purchasing patterns, revenue contribution, and opportunities for improving customer and sales performance.

## Workflow Pipeline
`Customers & Engineered Sales Data` -> `01_customer_sales_analysis.ipynb` -> `Customer Segmentation & Revenue Aggregation` -> `Segment & Regional Insights`

## Objectives
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior and revenue contribution
* Segment customers into High, Medium, and Low-value tiers based on purchasing volume
* Evaluate sales performance across customer types, industry segments, and regional channels
* Map revenue concentration risks to highlight high-value account dependencies
* Locate underperforming accounts and channel opportunities requiring operational focus
