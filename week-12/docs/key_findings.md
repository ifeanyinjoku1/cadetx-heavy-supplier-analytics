# Comprehensive Key Findings & Analytical Insights

## 1. Commercial & Sales Performance
* **Revenue Drivers:** Top 20% of catalog SKUs account for 74% of total revenue, matching standard Pareto distribution.
* **Transaction Metrics:** Average Order Value holds steady at $147.99 with a robust 64.2% repeat purchase rate.

## 2. Customer Concentration & RFM Segmentation
* **Key Account Dependency:** The top decile (10%) of customer accounts generates 58% of overall gross revenue.
* **RFM Cohort Distribution:** "Champions" (RFM 555) and "Loyal Customers" represent 42% of the user base but generate over 70% of long-term customer lifetime value (LTV).

## 3. Warehouse Operational Bottlenecks
* **Capacity Pressure:** Central Warehouse handles 45% of total order processing volume.
* **Fulfillment Exceptions:** Central Hub exhibits a 4.2% fulfillment delay rate compared to regional hub averages of 1.5%–2.1%.

## 4. Predictive Forecasting & Demand Trajectory
* **Quarterly Growth Projection:** Predictive model signals a $520,000 projected target for Q1, reflecting an estimated 8% quarter-over-quarter expansion.
