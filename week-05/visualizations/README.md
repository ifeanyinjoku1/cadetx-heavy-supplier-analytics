# Week 5 — Visualisations

This folder contains visualisations created for the Week 5 inventory analysis.

## Visualisations

- `top_max_stock_value.png` — Top products by maximum stock value.
- `abc_classification.png` — ABC classification based on maximum stock value.
- `lead_time_vs_safety_stock.png` — Relationship between lead time and safety stock.

## Purpose

The visualisations help communicate:

- Products with high stock-value requirements
- The distribution of products across ABC classes
- The relationship between supplier/product lead time and safety-stock requirements

These visuals support the Week 5 inventory health and stock-management analysis.
